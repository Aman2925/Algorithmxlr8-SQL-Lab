# Passed Every Subject

**Level:** `Level 3` · **Topic:** `Aggregation`

## Problem

The `Subjects` table lists the subjects of a course. The `Marks` table stores the marks students scored. Students can also have marks in subjects that are not part of the course.

A student passes the course when they have marks in every subject of the course and scored at least 60 in each of them.

Return `student_id` of every passing student, sorted from smallest to largest.
