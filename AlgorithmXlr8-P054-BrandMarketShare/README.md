**Level:** `Level 3` · **Topic:** `Aggregation`

## Problem

Report each brand's share of total revenue in percent. Brands with a share below 10 percent are combined into a single row named `Others`. If no brand is below 10 percent, there is no Others row. Round shares to 2 decimal places.

Return `brand` and `share_pct`, sorted by share_pct from highest to lowest and then by brand.
